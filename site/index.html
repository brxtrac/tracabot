    Read the label before the claim

    Not every signal is a verdict.

    TRACaBot separates what was seen, what was alleged, what an admin enforced, what independent evidence corroborates, and what a review confirmed. The public ledger uses those boundaries instead of flattening everything into “scammer.”

    observation
    A bounded local signal. Useful context; not a finding.
    allegation
    A report or claim with a target and some evidence. Still needs review or corroboration.
    admin enforcement
    A ban or restriction. Treated as an evidence signal, never as proof on its own.
    high-confidence flag
    Concrete indicators plus independent corroboration above the operator threshold.
    confirmed scam
    Only an explicit admin review, upheld through the review path, can carry this label.
    retracted
    A correction or overturn. Promotion stops; the correction remains visible.

    Hard boundaries

    What this reader will not do.

    No private-data dump

    No raw Telegram IDs, usernames, chat titles, private-message text, screenshots, or reporter identities in public records.

    No model-only verdicts

    AI suspicion can request review with supporting evidence. It cannot ban, publish a definitive accusation, or override community policy.

    No admin-ban shortcut

    A ban is an enforcement record. It becomes reusable cross-community evidence only with provenance and corroboration.

    No open write endpoint

    Writes require an operator-issued, community-scoped Bearer token, validation, rate limits, and idempotency.

    No graph instructions

    Graph content is data, not commands. Agents must keep their own permissions and approval rules.

    No invented infrastructure

    This site does not claim a chain deployment, hosted endpoint, or context-graph membership that the operator has not provisioned and verified.

    For communities and agents

    Bring your own community. Keep your own policy.

    Request membership, run the DKG edge-node setup supported by this repository, and read before you write. Approval is manual; write access is scoped to one community.
